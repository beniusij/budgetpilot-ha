# Changelog

## [0.3.47] — 2026-08-30

### Fixed — 2026-08-30

- **Dashboard category rows no longer lead to an empty page.** If your household splits costs proportionally, clicking a row under **Shared** on the Spending by category card opened the Transactions page with nothing on it — that page shows your own money for your household, so it had none of the shared payments the row was counting. Those rows are no longer clickable, and stop highlighting as though they were. Everything under **Personal**, and every row for households that pool income or nominate a payer, links as before.
