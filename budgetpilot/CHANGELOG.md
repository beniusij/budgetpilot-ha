# Changelog

## [0.3.35] — 2026-08-28

### Changed — 2026-08-28

- **The Transactions summary and the list beneath it now agree.** The figures at the top of the page counted one kind of money while the list below showed every transaction in the month, so amounts appeared in the list that none of the totals accounted for. Transactions now has two sides — **Household** and **Mine** — and whichever you are looking at decides both what the totals count and what the list shows. The page opens on the side that matches your household's money model, and every figure it has ever shown is unchanged; only the list narrowed to match. Your own spending keeps its own side, categories and all, so you can still see where your personal money goes.
- **Your partner's personal spending on a shared account is now labelled rather than silently uncounted.** It stays visible, because it moved the balance of an account you can both see, but it is dimmed, marked "Not counted here", and left out of every total — with a line under the figures saying so.
- **Household settings read in a more natural order.** Your target spending mix now sits directly under the income it is measured against, the keep-back settings lead the right-hand column, and the household members list has moved to the foot of the first column instead of sitting on its own below everything else.
