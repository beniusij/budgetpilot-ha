# Changelog

## [0.4.2] — 2026-09-17

### Fixed

- **The dashboard no longer greets you with the wrong time of day.** Leave Taupa open all day and the greeting kept whatever it said when you opened it — "Good morning" at half past nine at night. It now keeps up with the clock.
- **The dashboard moves into the new month on its own.** A window left open across midnight on the 1st stayed on the old month until you noticed and stepped forward. It now follows the change — unless you have deliberately stepped back to an earlier month, which is left where you put it.
- **Dates on the dashboard cards keep up with the day.** Leave Taupa open overnight and upcoming bills, contract renewals, account balances and the investments summary all went on measuring against yesterday — a bill due today still reading "Due tomorrow". They now move with the date.
