# Changelog

## [0.4.55] — 2026-11-05

### Added

- **Analytics now follows your needs, wants and savings month by month.** A chart under the spending chart shows each one in pounds, counted as on the Overview, with each one's target from your Settings income drawn as a dotted line beside it and its percentage at the end.

### Changed

- **On Analytics, point at the income line, or tap it, for any month's figure.** The line now has the Investments chart's look, and a box beside it shows the month's exact income.
