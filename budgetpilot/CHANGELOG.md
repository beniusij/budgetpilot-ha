# Changelog

## [0.4.35] — 2026-10-07

### Added

- **A short tour of setting Taupa up.** The first time you reach the Overview, Taupa walks you through the four things that get a household going — adding your accounts, shaping your categories, adding your bills and importing a statement — showing you the way to each page, and where to start once you are there. Close it to pick up later, skip it to stop for good, or take it again any time from the menu under your name.
- **Pro tips.** Now and then, when Taupa opens, a small card points out something it can do that is easy to miss — at most one every six hours, and only once you are through the setup tour. Turn them off under Settings → This device.
