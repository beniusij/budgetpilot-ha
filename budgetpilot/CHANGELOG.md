# Changelog

## [0.4.25] — 2026-10-06

### Fixed

- **A household that starts with one person gets shared categories when a partner joins.** Adding a partner used to give them only their own categories, leaving the household nothing joint to file rent, bills or groceries under. Joining now also adds shared Rent, Utilities, Groceries, Phone & Internet, Insurance and Transfers categories. The categories you already had stay as they are, with their history.
