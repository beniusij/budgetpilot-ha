# Changelog

## [0.3.36] — 2026-08-28

### Changed — 2026-08-28

- **Your keep-back is now the headline on the dashboard.** In a household that pools its income and keeps something back, the big figure at the top of the dashboard is what's yours to keep this period, with what was paid in underneath — and the shortfall note where a period's pay doesn't cover a flat keep-back. The separate "Your pay" panel that repeated the same figures lower down is gone. Every other household still leads with Free to spend, its income figure and the carry-forward pill, unchanged.
