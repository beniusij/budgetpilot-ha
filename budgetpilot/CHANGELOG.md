# Changelog

## [0.3.41] — 2026-08-29

### Added — 2026-08-29

- **Hover a Needs / Wants / Savings row to see what you spent.** The list under the dashboard's split bar now reads as plain proportions — Needs, Wants and Savings with their percentages — and the amount appears in a small balloon when you point at a row, given to the penny. Pointing at one row shows that band and no other, and the figure is reachable by keyboard as well as by mouse.
