# Changelog

## [0.3.25] — 2026-08-23

### Added — 2026-08-22

- **See who carries what, in a Designated Payer household.** The Bills page now shows each member's household bills side by side as a monthly figure, with the difference stated plainly — so an uneven split is visible instead of drifting quietly every time a bill changes. It is a comparison, not a debt: no money moves between you, and there is nothing to settle. If it looks lopsided, hand a bill to the other person.
- The bills register names who pays each household bill, and the dashboard's upcoming bills do too — so "£140 due Tuesday" tells you whose £140 it is.
- A household bill nobody is assigned to is now called out on all three, rather than quietly counting for no one.
