# Changelog

## [0.4.5] — 2026-09-26

### Added

- **Right-click a category on the dashboard.** In Spending by category, right-click a row to open a small menu. You can open that category's bills or transactions, already filtered to the same share (Personal or Shared) and category, or edit the category there instead of going to Settings. From the keyboard, move to a row with Tab and press Shift+F10. Clicking a row still opens its transactions, as it did before.
