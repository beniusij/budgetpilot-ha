# Changelog

## [0.3.23] — 2026-08-17

### Added — 2026-08-16

- Households that pool their money can now set a keep-back — the share of pay each member holds back rather than pooling — as a percentage or a fixed monthly amount, with a household default that any member can override for themselves.
- The dashboard now shows each member what their pay was this month and how much of it is theirs to keep.

### Changed — 2026-08-13

- **Bills you have budgeted nothing for this month no longer appear under Upcoming bills.** A bill kept on the register but set to £0 for the current month isn't a payment you are planning, so the dashboard card skips it and shows the next real commitment in its place. Amounts on the card now also reflect what the bill costs *this* month rather than its current headline price.
