# Changelog

## [0.4.11] — 2026-10-01

### Changed

- **Drag your import rules into order.** In Settings, each auto-categorisation rule now has a grip on its left. Drag it up or down and the rule moves with you, so taking a rule from the bottom of a long list to the top is one movement instead of one press per place. From the keyboard, move to a grip with Tab and press the up or down arrow. The small arrow buttons are gone. The order still matters in the same way: the first rule that matches wins. As before, nothing is saved until you press Save rules.
