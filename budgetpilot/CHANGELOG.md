# Changelog

## [0.3.26] — 2026-08-23

### Changed — 2026-08-23

- Tidier Settings: the Household tab is now two purposeful columns — what you enter about your pay on the left (income, split, payday), the rules applied to it on the right (target mix, income categories, keep-back) — with the Members card in a single column instead of stretching a short list across the page.
- The scope tag on a category now just reads "Personal" — the list only ever shows your own personal categories, so it no longer repeats your name on every row.
- Shorter wording on the auto-categorisation rules card.
