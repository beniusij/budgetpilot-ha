# Changelog

## [0.3.46] — 2026-08-30

### Changed — 2026-08-29

- **Scrollbars now match the rest of the app.** The browser's default grey slab has been replaced by a thin clay strip on a clear track, which brightens when you point at it. Scrolling by wheel, trackpad or keyboard is unchanged.
- **Pausing and deleting a bill have moved into the bill itself.** The three small icons at the end of each row appeared only when you pointed at it, and the first of them did what clicking the row already did. Open a bill and **Pause** and **Delete** now sit at the bottom of it, in plain sight and reachable by keyboard. Clicking a bill's name opens it too, so the register no longer needs a mouse at all.
- **The Household / Mine switch on Transactions is now only there for households that pool their income.** It was offering a choice the other two ways of splitting money do not make: if you each keep your own account and settle up, there is no household pot for a payment to belong to, so "the household's" and "yours" were two names for the same answer. The page now simply shows your own money, which is what the totals above the list have always counted. Shared payments are no longer listed on this page for those households — the Spending by category card on the dashboard is where to read them.
