# Changelog

## [0.3.41] — 2026-08-29

### Added — 2026-08-29

- **Hover a Needs / Wants / Savings bar to see the exact amount.** The split bar on the dashboard now shows a small balloon when you point at one of its three segments, giving that band's spend to the penny. The list underneath still rounds to whole pounds, so this is there for the moments you want the precise figure without hunting for it elsewhere. A band you have not spent anything on has no bar to point at — its figure stays readable in the list below, as before.
