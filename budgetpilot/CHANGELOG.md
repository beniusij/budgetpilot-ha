# Changelog

## [0.4.42] — 2026-10-10

### Added

- **Change month from the keyboard.** On the overview, the left and right arrow keys step back and forward a month, the same as the two buttons at the top. They are left alone while you are typing in a box.
- **A closed month says so on the overview.** When you look back at a month you have closed, a green mark under Free to Spend shows the month and the day you closed it. Click it to open that month in Reconcile.

### Changed

- **Looking back at a month shows what your accounts held then.** On a past month, the accounts beside Free to Spend show the balance each one was reconciled to at the end of that month, in place of today's. An account you didn't reconcile shows the app's own month-end figure and says "not reconciled". The strip of today's cash underneath is hidden while you look back.
- **A past month shows what it left over, not Free to Spend.** Looking back used to show a Free to Spend worked out from today's cash, which could be far more than you really had that month. It now reads "Left over in September" with the cash you ended the month on, less what you owed on cards, or "September ended short" in red.
- **Your accounts stay beside Free to Spend.** The panel next to the figure no longer changes every minute. The needs, wants and savings split and your goals now have their own cards just below, side by side, so everything is on the page at once. Click a goal there to edit it, or the card's title to open the Goals page.
- **Free to Spend is shown once.** The strip under the figure shows what it is worked out from (your cash, less what you owe on cards, less the planned spending left) without repeating the answer.
- **Plainer words around Free to Spend.** The explanation behind the small "i" is now two sentences instead of a paragraph. What last month handed on reads "left over from September", or "September ended short", in place of "carried in", and no longer has an arrow beside it that looked like a trend.

### Internal

- **The open question about Free to Spend on a past month is settled** and off the project board: a past month shows what it left over.
