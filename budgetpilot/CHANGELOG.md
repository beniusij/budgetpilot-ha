# Changelog

## [0.4.47] — 2026-10-18

### Added

- **Taupa can remember where a merchant goes.** After you file two or more transactions from the same merchant under a category in one go, Transactions asks "Always file TESCO STORES as Groceries?". Say yes and your future imports arrive with that category already filled in. It adds an ordinary rule to your own list in Settings › Rules, where you can change or delete it, and the confirmation has an Undo. Nothing is remembered unless you click, transactions already in Taupa are not touched, and your partner's rules are not affected.

### Changed

- **Personal free to spend holds back your share of the bills until you have moved it.** In a household that splits shared bills by percentage, the Personal figure used to count your share of the month's shared bills as spending money until you transferred it to the joint account, then drop by that amount. It now takes off a new line, "Still to pay into joint": your share of the month's shared budgets, less what you have already moved from your own accounts to the joint account. With £2,900 in the bank, £500 of personal budgets and a £1,200 share, Personal reads £1,200 before the transfer and £1,200 after. The amount is shown in the sum under the figure and in "How this is worked out", and it is never less than £0. Only a transfer Taupa has matched on both statements counts, so the line can still show an amount you have paid until the joint statement is imported; its explanation says so. A transfer made on or after your payday counts towards the next month, as it does for what a month left over, including when your pay landed early. The Shared figure and other household types are unchanged.
- **Reconcile says which way an account is off.** An account that is not reconciled used to read "-£5.00 off". It now says "bank balance is £5.00 lower than Taupa's" (or higher), and for a credit card "you owe £5.00 more than Taupa shows" (or less), with no minus sign.
- **Reconcile says which way a difference goes.** Under the account balances there is now a line saying a plus means your real balance is higher than Taupa's figure and a minus means it is lower, and that on a credit card or an overdraft higher means you owe less. While the month is open it also says to type a card or overdraft balance with a minus. The Calculated column has a hint saying it is the account's balance at the end of the last day of the month.
- **"Money in" on Transactions says what it counts.** The line under the figure read "Salary + refunds". It now reads "Your income categories", because the figure counts the income categories chosen in Settings, and a refund is not one unless you pick it.
- **Accepting a reconcile gap says what it does.** The Accept as-is tab no longer says the difference is "carried as-is". It says no transaction is added, Taupa's balance for the account stays different from your statement by the gap shown, the account counts as reconciled for that month, and the same gap shows again next month until you find the missing transaction or log an adjustment.
- **The hint beside a finished month's left-over says what the figure counts.** It now reads "What the joint current accounts held at the end of September, less what was owed on joint cards", and where money was counted towards the next month because of a pay day it says so: "Money a member paid in on or after their own payday counts towards October." The Personal hint says the same for your own pay and what you moved to the joint accounts. The guide to Free to spend has a new section on a month that has ended.
- **Reconcile shows what you are signing off.** The review step now lists each reconciled account with the statement balance you entered and the date it was reconciled. An account closed with a gap shows the gap and whether it was accepted or settled earlier. There is no total across accounts.
- **Reconcile shows the reconciled count once per step.** The "1 of 3 reconciled" chip in the header is gone; the line at the bottom of the accounts step keeps the count with what is left ("1 of 3 accounts reconciled · 1 discrepancy · 1 pending"), and a closed month now shows it too. One account reads "1 of 1 account reconciled", and a household with no accounts sees no count.
- **Reconcile changes month from the keyboard.** The left and right arrow keys step to the previous and next month, as they do on the overview. They stop at the current month and at your start month, and do nothing while you are typing in a balance box.
- **Settings income is now labelled take-home pay.** The field reads "Take-home pay (£ a month)" or "(£ a year)" with the note "After tax and deductions." If you entered your salary before tax, replace it with what reaches your account. No figure changes until you do.
- **The Bills figures say what they are.** The three tiles are now "Shared bills", "Your share" and "Your personal bills", each marked with the month they are for, and the hints say plainly that yearly and quarterly bills are spread across the year, weekly bills are counted for the month, and the figure is a cost rather than money leaving your account. The word "normalised" is gone from the tiles and from Who carries what.
- **Settings says what its money wording means.** The split card now reads "You pay 58% of shared bills, Ben pays 42%", and says the percentages are rounded when they are worked out from your incomes. The pay schedule options read "Count pay in the month it arrives", "Paid on a set day" and "Paid on the last weekday", with a line saying pay on or after the day counts towards next month and how to cover an early payday. The keep-back options read "% of each person's pay" and "£ a month each", and say 0 means everything is pooled. The Needs / Wants / Savings card says it is the share of income you aim to put to each. Income says it is a planning figure, and income, the split and a category budget say which month a change applies from.

- **The month for a payday override is picked from a list.** In Settings, adding an early payday used to mean typing the month as "2026-12", and a slip left the Add button greyed out with no reason given. You now choose the month by name, from this month to eleven months ahead.

### Fixed

- **Switching your income to Annual no longer changes the other person's income.** In Settings, Monthly or Annual is now chosen for each person's take-home pay on its own. Before, one switch applied to both of you: with £2,600 and £1,900 a month, one of you switching to Annual and typing £31,200 made the other's £1,900 count as a year's pay, and the split of shared bills moved from 58% / 42% to 94% / 6%. Switching now also converts your own figure for you (£2,600 a month becomes £31,200 a year), so nothing moves until you type a new amount. Past months keep the period they were saved with. If your figure was already saved against the wrong period, type it in again.
- **A payday on a weekend or bank holiday no longer puts two salaries in one month.** If you are paid on a set day or the last weekday and your pay arrives up to 4 days early, it now counts as that payday's pay, towards the next month, without you adding the date yourself. Before, a salary paid on Friday 23 October for the 24th counted as October's, so October showed two salaries and November none. A move to the joint account made on or after the day that pay arrived goes with it. Earlier months with an early payday change to match, on the overview and in Reconcile. If you are paid more than 4 days early, as many are in December, you still add that payday in Settings. One thing to know: if your household has no start month and the last month you closed had an early payday, this month's Free to Spend can read one salary too high until you reopen that month and close it again.
- **A refund is no longer counted as income by default.** Until you choose your income categories in Settings, Taupa guesses them from their names. It used to pick a category named "Refunds" along with your salary and interest, which made your income look higher by money you had only got back. It now picks salary and interest only. If you have already chosen your income categories, nothing changes, and you can still tick a refunds category yourself.
- **The Categories list adds up.** In Settings, each category's Bills, Buffer and Total budget are now shown to the penny, so the two parts always add up to the total beside them. Before, each was rounded to whole pounds, and £33.50 plus £10.50 read as £34 + £11 = £44.
- **You both see the same Shared left-over when you each pay in at month end.** Money a member pays into the joint accounts on or after their own payday counts towards the next month, like the pay it came from. This used to apply only to your own payment, so with £150 in the joint account and £1,200 and £800 paid in on 30 September, one of you read £950 left over and the other £1,350. Now you both read £150, and October shows the same. It needs each of you to have linked the transfer and set a pay day in Settings. When one of you is paid a few days early, their payment follows the day the pay actually arrived for both of you: with pay due on the 24th arriving on Friday the 23rd and £800 paid in that day, you both read £150, where the other member used to read £950.
- **A payday moved into another month no longer misdates that month's transfers.** If one month's pay was set to land in a different month, such as December's pay arriving on 27 November, every transfer you made to the joint accounts in December was counted towards January, so December's left-over missed them all. With pay set to land late, on 2 January, none was moved. Now the transfers in that month are dated by your usual pay day.
- **Changing a setting no longer marks everyone's settings as changed.** Saving one person's setting used to re-save the other person's settings and the household's as well, so two people each changing their own settings on different devices would be asked to choose between them when the devices synced. Now a save only touches what it actually changed.
- **A small price change no longer shows as "+£0".** On Bills, a rise or fall under £1 now shows its pence, such as "+£0.40", instead of a coloured zero.

### Internal

- **The roadmap board marks the same Shared left-over for both members as done.** The Phase 1 board has a Done card for each member's payment into the joint accounts following their own pay day, the day early pay landed, and the left-over hint wording.
- **The roadmap board marks the income period per member as done.** The Phase 1 board has a Done card for each member choosing Monthly or Annual for their own income, and the Settings feedback entry notes that follow-up as done.
- **The roadmap board notes the clearer Accept as-is wording.** The Reconcile card on the Phase 1 board now says the Accept as-is tab states what accepting does: no transaction is added, and the gap stays and shows again next month.
- **The Reconcile notes agree on the match rule.** The product note for the Reconcile page said a gap was flagged only beyond a tolerance; it now says, as the app does, that balances must match to the penny. New tests pin which way the Difference sign goes for a current account and for a credit card.
